The Five Foundations

The Kompresorinė Protocol is not an administrative checklist — it is a set of operational commitments that define what kind of institution Kompresorinė is, and how it will behave regardless of which space it inhabits.

Protocol rules are intentionally few and intentionally strong. They are designed to migrate with the institution into Phase II, providing continuity across sites and governance transitions.
