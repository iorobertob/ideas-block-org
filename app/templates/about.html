About this platform

This governance platform operationalizes the Ideas Block – Kompresorinė and LMTA artistic research satellite model. It translates the proposal into a working management system for governance, research scheduling, budgeting, partnership development, risk control, public dissemination, protocol implementation, and Phase II transfer planning.

  • Governance: steering group records, policies, user roles, monthly reports.
  • Research infrastructure: projects, bookings, dissemination, documentation-minded workflow.
  • Financial and technical continuity: budget ledger, portable equipment inventory, transfer planning.
  • Phase II: successor-site roadmap, national network building, partnership requests.
  • Protocol: codified Kompresorinė operational rules that can migrate to future spaces.
