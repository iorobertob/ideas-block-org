LMTA Mokslo centras – Ideas Block Kompresorinė
Artistic Research Satellite (2026–2029)
Executive Brief, Concept Proposal, and Kompresorinė Protocol


1. Executive Brief
This document proposes a collaboration between the LMTA Mokslo centras (Science and Research Centre) and Ideas Block – Kompresorinė to establish a temporary artistic research platform operating for a three-year period (2026–2029).

The platform would function as an external artistic research satellite supporting practice-led inquiry, rehearsal-as-research, experimentation, prototyping, and research dissemination.

Rather than investing in permanent building infrastructure, the project prioritizes portable technical equipment, operational systems for artistic research activity, documentation and archiving methods, research coordination and management, and a transferable infrastructure model.

Although the Kompresorinė building is expected to be demolished within approximately three years, the infrastructure created during the pilot would remain portable and transferable to a future site.

Key outcomes include dedicated working space for artistic researchers, environments for experimentation and rehearsal-as-research, dissemination formats connecting research and audiences, documentation of artistic research processes, and a tested model for artistic research infrastructure in Lithuania.


2. Concept Proposal
Artistic research frequently requires spatial conditions that differ from conventional academic environments. 
Processes such as sonic experimentation, performative investigation, rehearsal-as-research, and scenographic prototyping require flexible working environments that allow for iterative exploration.

The proposed partnership between LMTA Mokslo centras and Ideas Block – Kompresorinė aims to establish a structured environment capable of supporting these research processes while remaining institutionally reliable and professionally governed.


Program Model
The platform operates through two interconnected layers.

Research Infrastructure Layer:
• Research residencies
• Practice-based experimentation sessions
• Rehearsal-as-research laboratories
• Sonic and spatial testing
• Peer review and reflection sessions
• Documentation and archiving

Public Dissemination Layer:
• Lecture-performances
• Research demonstrations
• Open rehearsals
• Public discussion formats
• Research presentations and festivals


Program Pillars
Research Studio – Flexible rehearsal and experimentation space.

Research Development – Workshops and seminars addressing artistic research methodology and documentation.

Research Presentation – Public formats designed to communicate research processes.

Legacy and Transfer – Planning for continuation of the platform beyond the demolition of the building.


3. Phase II: Post-Kompresorinė Development (2029+)
The Kompresorinė platform is conceived as a pilot phase for the development of a long-term artistic research infrastructure associated with LMTA and its wider research ecosystem.

By the end of the pilot phase the project aims to produce a tested operational model, portable technical infrastructure, documentation systems, and a community of artist-researchers.

Possible Phase II developments include:

• Permanent artistic research laboratory affiliated with LMTA
• Hybrid university–independent research platform
• National artistic research infrastructure supporting multiple Lithuanian institutions


4. The Kompresorinė Protocol
The Kompresorinė Protocol is a set of operational principles developed during the pilot phase describing how artistic research environments can function effectively.

Key elements include:

Spatial Flexibility – spaces shift between rehearsal, lab, seminar, and presentation modes.

Research-First Scheduling – time allocation prioritizes research processes.

Documentation Culture – research activities are documented through video, audio, and written reflection.

Public Research Interface – public activities function as dissemination of research.

Portable Infrastructure – equipment systems remain transferable.

Governance Transparency – clear decision-making and reporting structures.


5. Conclusion
The Kompresorinė Artistic Research Satellite represents an opportunity to address current spatial challenges while developing a transferable model for artistic research infrastructure.

Although the building itself is temporary, the systems, knowledge, equipment, and partnerships developed through the project will continue beyond its demolition, forming the basis for future artistic research platforms.
